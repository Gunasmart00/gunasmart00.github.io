Privacy Policy

Applies to: Lullaby Guard (Android and web versions). Last updated: 2 October 2026.

Shooting Star Games ("we") makes games for children and families. We designed Lullaby Guard to collect as little information as possible. This policy explains what happens to data when you play.

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no chat, and no way to share personal information in the game.
  • Your progress (stars, coins, settings) is saved on your device.
  • On Android, we ask the player's birth year once and store only an age group (child, teen or adult) — never the birth year.
  • The game shows ads. For children, ads are never personalised.

Information stored on your device

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device (on Android, in the app's own storage; on the web, in your browser's storage). We do not receive this data. Deleting the app or clearing your browser data removes it. On CrazyGames, logged-in players' progress may be saved to their CrazyGames account by CrazyGames.

Age screen (Android)

When the Android app is first opened, it asks "When were you born?". We use the answer only to choose the right advertising settings and store only the resulting age group (under 13, 13–15, or 16 and over). Skipping the question is treated as under 13. A grown-up can change the answer later in Settings, behind a parent check.

Advertising

Android: ads are provided by Google AdMob.

  • Players under 13: ad requests are marked as child-directed (COPPA) and limited to "G"-rated content. Ads are not personalised and the advertising ID is not used for personalisation.
  • Players 13–15: ad requests are marked as under the age of consent, so ads are not personalised, and are limited to "PG"-rated content.
  • Players 16 and over: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states), Google's consent form asks for permission before any personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.

Google may collect device information (such as the advertising ID, IP address and app usage) to deliver and measure ads. See how Google uses data from partner apps.

Web portals: when you play on Poki, CrazyGames or GameDistribution, ads are delivered by that portal under its own privacy policy. Rewarded ads are always optional: the player chooses to watch them.

Purchases

If a "Remove Ads" purchase is offered, it is processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Purchases are behind a parent check for players under 13.

Game settings download (Android)

The Android app may download a small settings file (for example, ad frequency limits) from our website. Like any web request, this reveals your IP address to our web host (GitHub Pages). We do not log or store it.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game has no accounts, chat or social features. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Turn sound and music off, or switch on Little Helper mode (no ads), in Settings.
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings.
  • Delete all game data by uninstalling the app or clearing your browser data.
